✅ Step 1: Create a New AR Project

Open **Xcode**.
Go to File > New > **Project**.
Select **Augmented Reality App** under the iOS section.

P.S- Brace yourself. Xcode is a shitshow. They have not provided a good brief to the designer or dont have a designer in apple who has paid attention to interaction design principles in design school. 

✅ Step 2: Details for New AR Project

**Product Name:** Choose something like ARModelViewer.
**Interface:** Storyboard or SwiftUI (SwiftUI is modern and recommended).
**Language:** Swift.
**Content Technology:** Select **RealityKit** (easier for USDZ models) or **SceneKit** (if you want more control).


✅ Step 3: Create a repository for the Project

Make sure it is checked.
